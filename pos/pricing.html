Frequently asked questions

Clear answers to common questions about choosing and using POS software.

Is Puravigal POS free to start?

The current pricing page presents a free starting experience. Review the page and signup flow for the exact terms and limits that apply.

How much is the Puravigal POS Standard plan?

The current page lists ₹199 per month or ₹1,999 per year. Check the live pricing page for current terms before purchase.

Should I choose monthly or yearly POS billing?

Monthly billing can suit businesses still evaluating a product. Yearly billing may reduce the effective monthly cost when you are confident the workflow fits. Compare the displayed totals before deciding.

What should I compare besides the POS subscription price?

Include hardware, setup, staff training, support, integrations and any additional fees needed for your actual workflow.
