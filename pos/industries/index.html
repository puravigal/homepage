Helpful answers

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers to common questions about choosing and using POS software.

Can one POS product support different business types?

A shared POS foundation can support different businesses when the product catalogue, billing flow, stock process and reporting fit each business's real needs.

Which business types does Puravigal POS cover on this site?

The current business-type pages cover retail shops, restaurants, cafés, mobile shops, supermarkets and grocery or provision stores.

Should I choose POS software by industry or by features?

Use both: choose a product that fits your industry workflow, then confirm it supports the billing, stock, purchase, reporting and hardware requirements you actually have.

How can I compare POS products for my business?

Write down your checkout steps, stock rules, payment methods, reporting needs, devices and budget, then test those requirements against the vendor's current product.
