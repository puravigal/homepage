Questions answered

Frequently asked questions

Useful points to consider when choosing POS software for this type of business.

What should POS software track in a mobile shop?

A mobile shop typically needs clear product records, pricing, accessory inventory, billing, purchase history and visibility into daily sales.

Can POS software support both phones and accessories?

A POS product catalogue can organise different product categories, provided the software supports the catalogue and stock workflows your shop needs. Check current product capabilities before migrating.

Why is inventory important for mobile retailers?

Devices and accessories can have different prices and stock levels. Accurate product and purchase records help reduce manual tracking and make stock easier to review.

What should I check before choosing mobile shop POS software?

Review product catalogue flexibility, billing speed, inventory handling, purchase records, reports, hardware support and pricing. Confirm any serial-number or IMEI tracking requirement directly with the vendor.
