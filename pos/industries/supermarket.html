Questions answered

Frequently asked questions

Useful points to consider when choosing POS software for this type of business.

What matters most in supermarket POS software?

Key considerations include fast checkout, a well-organised product catalogue, inventory visibility, purchase workflows, reporting and compatibility with the store's hardware.

Can small supermarkets use the same POS as retail shops?

They can if the product supports the supermarket's catalogue size, checkout process, stock practices and hardware requirements. Validate these needs before choosing.

Should I check barcode and receipt-printer support?

Yes. Confirm scanner, barcode, printer and other peripheral compatibility with the exact devices and workflow your store uses.

How do I evaluate supermarket POS pricing?

Compare the full cost of the plan, hardware, onboarding, support and any required integrations rather than comparing the subscription price alone.
