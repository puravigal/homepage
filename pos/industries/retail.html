Frequently asked questions

Useful points to consider when choosing POS software for this type of business.

What should retail POS software help a shop manage?

Retail POS software should connect product billing with inventory, purchases, customer records and sales reporting so daily transactions are easier to track.

Is Puravigal POS designed for small retail shops?

Puravigal POS is being developed for micro and small businesses, including retail shops that need a practical workflow for products, stock and everyday sales.

Can I use one POS for billing and stock management?

Puravigal POS brings billing, products and inventory into one workflow. Review the current features page for the capabilities available in the current release.

How do I compare POS software for my retail store?

Compare billing speed, stock visibility, purchase handling, customer records, reports, support and the total cost for the way your shop operates.
