Privacy Policy

How Puravigal handles information across our website, accounts and products.

Last updated: 8 October 2026

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Puravigal website and, where applicable, Puravigal accounts and products such as Puravigal POS. Product-specific notices may provide additional information for a particular service.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use Puravigal, we may collect information such as your name, business name, email address, phone number, account details, support enquiries and information you choose to provide about your business.

3. Product information

When you use a Puravigal product, information you enter may be processed to provide the features you request. For Puravigal POS, this may include business, product, sales, inventory and customer information where those features are used.

4. How we use information

We may use information to create and manage accounts, provide product functionality, respond to enquiries, provide support, improve services, communicate important product information, maintain security and meet legal obligations.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may use trusted service providers to host, secure, operate or support Puravigal services. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect our services and users.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the services we provide. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, product operation, support, security, legal requirements and legitimate business needs.

8. Your choices

You may contact us regarding access, correction or clarification of personal information associated with your account or enquiry, subject to applicable law and reasonable verification requirements.

9. Product communications

We may send service-related communications such as account, security, support or product-release information. Marketing communications, where used, will provide an appropriate way to manage those communications.

10. Changes to this policy

As Puravigal and its products evolve, this policy may be updated. The latest version will be published on this page with the applicable update date.

11. Contact

Privacy questions: support@puravigal.com.
