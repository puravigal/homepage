About Puravigal

We build software for the businesses behind everyday life.

Puravigal is a software platform focused on practical tools for micro and small businesses. Our first product is POS, with a broader suite planned over time.

Our approach

Useful first. Complex only when necessary.

Many small businesses do not need a giant software system. They need billing that works, sales they can understand, stock they can track and software their team can learn quickly.

That is the problem Puravigal is designed to solve. We focus on clear workflows, sensible features and accessible products that can grow with the business.

POS is the beginning. Over time, Puravigal will bring connected applications such as CRM and ERP into one broader business ecosystem.
